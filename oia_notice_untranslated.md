## International Student Exchange Program (ISEP) - US and Multi (기타)

- ISEP (International Student Exchange Program) is not a single university but a global non-profit network of around 300 universities. Through ISEP you list several preferred host universities and are placed at the best available option according to space and your academic profile; Yonsei students are guaranteed a placement within their top three choices, provided they meet the academic requirements. Restricted study areas and the language of instruction vary by host university, and some options require the host to run on a semester system. Please consult OIA and the ISEP host list when applying.

## GE3 (기타)

- GE3 (Global Engineering Education Exchange) is not a single university but a global consortium of engineering schools administered by the Institute of International Education (IIE), with around 70 member universities in more than 20 countries. It is open to undergraduate engineering students only. Through GE3 you study for a semester or a full year at a member university abroad while continuing to pay tuition at Yonsei. The available host universities and their course offerings vary, so please review the GE3 member list and consult OIA when choosing a destination.
